Every look and tag

The big number is the average rating, 1 to 7, that each of you gave the faces with that look or tag. The small one is how far that sits from the same person's average over every face. “Higher by” is the difference between the two small numbers, under it the name of whoever rates it higher.

A gap that is more than chance is not a reason. The goths wear bold makeup, so one taste shows up in several rows.

A look with six or seven faces needs a gap of two points or more before it can be told from chance; a tag that fifty faces have needs about one.

The marks allow for how widely each of you spreads your ratings: someone who uses all of 1 to 7 sits further from their own average on everything, with the same taste.

When both of you give a look your lowest ratings, the one whose average is lower comes out “higher”: there was less room to fall. Read the big numbers first.
